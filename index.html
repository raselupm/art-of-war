Squad Commander – Draw the Way, Win the War

Squad Commander is a free strategy puzzle game you play in your browser. Draw a path from your army to the prisoner, choose the safest route past enemy squads, and win battles automatically with swordsmen, archers and knights.

Features

  • 50 levels with forks, spirals, river bridges and grid mazes
  • Boss battles against the Warlord every 10 levels
  • Enemy archers, brutes, knights, archer towers and cannons
  • Explosive barrels, spike traps, lava pools and landmines
  • Free caged allies and collect coins to upgrade your army
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktop. No download needed.
